This update is about fitting your life more precisely into the plan. Budgets are easier to read and to order, shared expenses finally land in your own budget, and yearly expenses march to the rhythm of your own calendar. There’s a healthy dose of polish too — a split helper that finishes the math for you, a keyboard that stops covering your fields, and budgets you can tap straight into.

- The "Available" amount on the overview screen no longer overflows out of its bubble — it fits neatly and stays readable
- Fresh budget colors, picked to be much easier to tell apart at a glance
- Budgets can now be reordered, and the new order is mirrored everywhere — overview, planning and transactions
- Shared expenses now support notes and attachments, just like your personal transactions
- A shared expense can be assigned to one of your personal budgets, so it shows up in your own planning automatically
- Shared expenses stay inside the group’s period — you can no longer add entries dated outside of it
- Yearly expenses follow a flexible time frame, so you decide when the year starts and ends
- Subscriptions were retested end to end, and the rough edges that surfaced are fixed
- "Add rest" for exact and percentage shares — one tap fills the last share for you automatically
- The keyboard no longer overlays the fields you’re typing into — the screen scrolls up so you always see what you’re entering
- Tap a budget on the overview to see every transaction assigned to it
- When planning a budget you now see how much of your allowance remains after expected fixed expenses and your other budgets, in both the default and the current month

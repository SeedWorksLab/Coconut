### Your account in Settings

- With cloud sync enabled, a new account card at the top of Settings shows your username, Seed profile picture, and email. Tap it to open Cloud settings.
- Sign in to an existing Seed account in Coconut, or follow the registration link to create one on the SeedWorks website.

### Faster, more reliable sync

- Faster downloads and local data updates, especially when restoring your account on the web.
- Less repeated background work and quicker uploads after edits.
- Improved protection for pending edits and more reliable retries for interrupted uploads and missing data links.

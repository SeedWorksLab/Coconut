### Coconut has its own home on the web

Coconut gets a dedicated web address: **https://coconut.seedworkslab.de/**.
Sign in with your SeedWorks account to access your Coconut data in the browser.
If you are already signed in centrally, you can continue without entering your
password again while that session remains valid.

- Secure central sign-in with separate app sessions and explicit authorization.
- Manage and revoke Coconut’s access from your SeedWorks account page.
- New stable releases deploy the web app automatically.
- Android and desktop remain account-optional and local-first.

### More reliable subscription sync

- Saved monthly subscription charges sync between devices, alongside their
  subscription definitions.
- Historical charge amounts are preserved rather than replaced with a
  subscription’s current price.

### A smoother web sign-in

- Signed-in web users go straight to their account’s data instead of seeing the
  first-run choice to start fresh or sign in.
- Initial account restoration downloads existing cloud data before enabling
  background uploads.

### Visible Android update downloads

- APK downloads use Android’s system progress and completion notification.
- Completed downloads can be handed to the Android installer.

### Desktop account visibility

- Desktop connections identify themselves as **Coconut (desktop)** in your Seed
  account.
- Connection reporting is attempted even when a later data upload fails.

### Switching to the new web address

- Use the same SeedWorks account; successfully synced data downloads from
  SeedWorks Cloud after sign-in.
- Browser storage and login sessions do not transfer between the old website
  address and the new subdomain. Make sure any pending edits have synced before
  switching.
- Signing out of Coconut is separate from signing out of the central account.
  Revoking access prevents token refresh; existing access tokens may remain
  valid until they expire.
